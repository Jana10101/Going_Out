# Going Out 🧭

**Discover Egypt beyond the postcard.**

Going Out is our final project for the DEPI Digital Marketing track. We're building the full marketing plan for a domestic tourism app that helps Egyptians and visitors explore *all* of Egypt, not just the same five famous cities.

---

## 💡 Project Idea

Most Egyptians who want to travel inside the country end up booking through random Facebook pages they don't fully trust. Foreign tourists get the opposite problem: the big global platforms only show them the pyramids, Luxor and a couple of beaches.

Going Out sits in the middle. It's one app, made in Egypt for Egypt, where you can:

- **Pick a ready-made trip** or **build your own**: choose your start and end points, transport, hotel and an optional tour guide, and see the full price at the end
- **Explore every governorate**, including hidden places most people have never heard of
- **Travel with peace of mind**: every user is verified (national ID for Egyptians, passport for visitors), there's a report system, and every hotel and venue is alcohol-free and family-friendly
- **Earn points** by filling in a short survey and sharing a photo after each trip, then spend them on rewards or a free trip
- **Save with group discounts**, get nearby-place alerts from sponsors, and watch short videos of each place before you go

Our core message, from the Golden Circle:

> *People don't always need a new destination. Sometimes they need a new experience.*

**Who it's for:** young Egyptians (18–34), international tourists, Gulf and Arab families, older Egyptians, and companies looking for organized employee trips (B2B).

## 🗓️ Work Plan

We split the project into milestones and worked through them in order:

| Phase | What we did | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Research** | Problem, objectives, secondary research, PEST, SWOT, competitor analysis, and hands-on testing of competing apps | ✅ Done |
| **2. Business foundations** | Business Model Canvas and Golden Circle, built together as a team | ✅ Done |
| **3. Strategy** | STP, market analysis, SOSTAC marketing plan, social media platforms plan | ✅ Done (Milestone 1) |
| **4. Content calendar** | 3-week pre-launch teaser across TikTok, Instagram and Facebook, later trimmed to the 3 strongest posts a week so we can actually deliver it | 🔄 In progress |
| **5. Execution** | Post the teaser, run one paid ad in Week 1, track results weekly | ⏳ Next |

The app itself isn't built yet, so the content is a **teaser only**: we're building curiosity and an audience with no download link and no launch date. A Pharaonic cat mascot appears in small hints along the way and is fully revealed at the end.

The SOSTAC plan also lays out what happens after launch: 2 months of pre-launch, an influencer and ads wave in Month 1, the points program and user photos in Months 2–3, then B2B outreach and more governorates in Months 4–6.

## 📊 KPIs

**For the teaser phase (tracked weekly):**

- Followers gained
- Average watch time
- Shares and sends
- Saves
- Comments
- Hashtag uses (#GoingOut)

For a teaser, these tell us much more than likes do.

**After launch (from our SOSTAC plan):**

| KPI | Target |
|---|---|
| Reach | 1 million people in the first 3 months |
| Downloads | 50,000 in 6 months (about 70% Egyptians, 30% visitors) |
| Activation | 30% of new users build or book a trip in their first month |
| 30-day retention | 20% |
| Trips booked | 5,000 in 6 months |
| Partners | 30 tourism companies and halal hotels + 50 sponsored places, in at least 10 governorates |
| B2B | 10 companies signed for employee trips in the first year |
| App rating | 4.5 stars or higher |
| Customer acquisition cost | Lower every month |

These are our own estimates for a new app. We'd adjust them once real launch data comes in.
